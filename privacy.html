The short version: Glow does all of its work on your Mac. Your camera video and your face data never leave your Mac. Glow does not store them, and it does not send them to us or to anyone else. We collect no data about you.

This policy explains how the Glow Camera app for Mac ("Glow", "the app") handles information. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the developer of Glow Camera. It covers the Glow app and the Glow Camera virtual camera that the app adds to your Mac.

Data we collect

None. Glow does not collect, store or share personal data. In detail:

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics and no tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party SDKs or third-party code.
  • No servers. Glow does not send your video, images, face data or settings anywhere.

When you buy Glow, Apple handles the purchase. We do not receive your name or contact details from the App Store.

On-device processing

Glow reads frames from your camera, applies the effects you choose, and sends the result to the Glow Camera virtual camera on your Mac. Video apps such as Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and FaceTime then read Glow Camera like any other camera.

All of this happens on your Mac, in memory. This includes skin smoothing, lighting, face shape, makeup, teeth whitening, eye brightening, and background blur and replacement. Glow does not save camera frames to disk. Each frame is discarded after it is shown in the preview and passed to Glow Camera.

To blur or replace your background, Glow finds the outline of the person in each frame with Apple's Vision framework, on your Mac. This outline (a person mask) is used only for that frame, in memory, and then discarded. It is not stored or shared.

After that, the video app you use decides what happens to the video. For example, it may send it to the people on your call. That app's own privacy policy applies to it.

Face data

Glow needs to know where your face is to place its effects. This section explains exactly what it uses and why.

What is processed
Face geometry: the positions of face landmark points (for example the outline of your face, eyes, lips and mouth) in each camera frame. Glow computes these points with Apple's Vision framework, which runs on your Mac.
Purpose
Only to place effects on the right part of the image, such as lip color on your lips, whitening on your teeth, and smoothing on your skin.
Where
On your Mac only. Face data never leaves your Mac.
Storage
Not stored. The points exist in memory only while Glow processes a frame. Then they are discarded.
Retention
0 days. Glow keeps no face data after the frame is processed.
Sharing
Not shared with us, with Apple or with any third party.
Other uses
None. Glow does not use face data to identify you or anyone else, to recognize faces, to track you, for advertising, or for analytics. It does not build a face profile.

Camera access

macOS asks for your permission before Glow can use your camera. Glow uses the camera only to make the filtered video for the preview and for Glow Camera.

Glow turns your camera on only while the Glow window is open or a video app is using Glow Camera. The rest of the time the camera is off.

You can turn camera access off at any time in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera.

Settings and background picture on your Mac

Glow saves your settings, such as your chosen look and slider positions, on your Mac using the standard macOS preferences system (UserDefaults). Settings do not include camera images or face data.

If you choose a picture to replace your background, Glow saves a copy of that picture in its own folder on your Mac, so it is still there after a restart. Glow uses it only as your background.

Settings and the background picture stay on your Mac. Glow does not send them anywhere.

Crash reports and diagnostics

Crash and hang reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple can deliver crash and hang reports for Glow to the app on your Mac, through Apple's MetricKit framework. Glow keeps the last 10 of these reports on your Mac. It does not send them anywhere by itself. They are included only in a Copy Diagnostics report that you choose to send (see below).

If you share analytics with app developers, Apple may also give us anonymous crash data through App Store Connect. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy. You can change this choice in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Copy Diagnostics

If you need help, you can choose Glow > Settings > Copy Diagnostics. This puts a short text report on your clipboard. Nothing is sent. You decide whether to paste it into an email to us.

The report contains:

  • The Glow and macOS versions, and your Mac model.
  • Setup state, for example whether Glow Camera is turned on and whether camera access is allowed.
  • The names of your cameras.
  • Recent Glow log messages.
  • Summaries of the crash reports described above.

The report never contains images or face data. If you email it to us, we use it only to answer your request, and we do not share it.

Children

Glow does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 or under the age of digital consent where you live.
