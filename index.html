Look your best on every call.

Glow Camera is a webcam filter for your Mac. Smooth skin, better light and a touch of makeup in Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime and more. Everything runs on your Mac.

How it works

Glow adds a camera called Glow Camera to your Mac. You choose it in your video app, just like any other camera.

  1. Open Glow. A short checklist helps you allow the camera and turn on Glow Camera.
  2. Pick a look. Choose Natural, Soft, Studio or Glam, then fine-tune what you like.
  3. Choose Glow Camera. In Zoom, Meet, Teams, FaceTime, Slack, Webex or OBS, pick Glow Camera as your camera.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Subtle by default. Every effect has a slider, so you decide how much.

Looks

Natural, Soft, Studio and Glam. One click each, or press ⌘1 to ⌘4.

Skin that still looks like skin

Edge-aware smoothing evens out skin and keeps its real texture. Adjust brightness and warmth too.

Virtual lighting

Add two virtual lights to light your face the way you like.

Face shape

Slim your face a little, or make your eyes a little bigger.

Makeup

Lip color in 6 shades or any custom shade. Teeth whitening that keeps lips and gums natural. Eye brightening.

Background blur and replacement

Blur a messy room, or replace your background.

Compare

Hold Space or the compare button to see the original. Only your preview changes. People on the call still see the filtered image.

On or off in a moment

Turn all filters on or off with ⌘E, the toolbar or the menu bar icon.

Stays out of the way

Glow waits in the menu bar. Your camera turns on only while the Glow window is open or a video app uses Glow Camera.

Private by design

Glow does all of its work on your Mac. Your video and your face never leave it.

  • Camera frames are processed on your Mac and never stored.
  • Nothing is sent to any server.
  • Face points are used only to place effects, then discarded.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no third-party code.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases.
