How it works

Glow Camera shows up in your video app's camera menu next to your webcam. Glow reads your real camera, applies your settings and passes the result to Glow Camera.

What you can adjust

Start with one of four looks, then change any setting. Glow remembers your choices.

Looks
Natural, Soft, Studio and Glam. Switch with ⌘1 to ⌘4.
Skin
Smoothing that blurs only the skin and leaves some texture, plus brightness and warmth for the whole picture.
Lighting
Two lights you drag around your face. Each has its own strength, warmth and spread.
Face shape
Slim face and bigger eyes, both off by default.
Makeup
Lip color in six shades or any color you pick, teeth whitening that leaves lips and gums alone, and brighter eyes.
Background
Blur the room, or replace it with a color or one of your photos.
Compare
Hold Space to see your unfiltered camera in Glow. The call keeps the filtered picture. ⌘E turns all filters off for everyone.
More than one person
Face effects follow up to four faces in the frame.
Output
640 × 360, 1280 × 720 or 1920 × 1080 at 30 frames per second, whichever your video app asks for.
Languages
English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Brazilian Portuguese.

Privacy

Glow processes each frame in memory on your Mac and then discards it. Your video and face data are never stored or uploaded.

Read the privacy policy

  • No account, sign-in, analytics, ads or third-party code.
  • Face points are used to place effects in one frame, then thrown away.
  • The camera is on only while Glow's window is open or a video app is using Glow Camera.
  • Glow has no servers. Settings stay on your Mac.

Requirements

  • Mac with Apple siliconM1 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.
  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • A cameraBuilt-in, USB, or an iPhone through Continuity Camera.

Questions

Is there a subscription?

No. Glow is a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store. There is no account to create.

Does my video go anywhere?

Only to the video app you choose Glow Camera in, the same as with any camera. Glow itself doesn't keep or upload anything. The privacy policy has the details.

Glow Camera doesn't show up in Zoom or Meet

Quit the video app with ⌘Q and open it again. An app that was already open when Glow Camera was installed can't see it until it restarts. For Meet, restart Chrome. More on this.

Does Glow need to stay open?

Yes. Glow Camera shows your video only while Glow is running. Turn on "Open Glow when you log in" and it waits in the menu bar.
