How it works

A camera like any other. Just a better one.

Glow adds a camera called Glow Camera to your Mac. Your video app sees it in its camera menu, next to your webcam. Set it up once, and every call looks the way you want.

Everything you need. Nothing overdone.

Start from a look, then fine-tune. Every effect keeps your real skin texture and fades in gently, so you look like yourself on a good day.

Four looks

Natural, Soft, Studio and Glam. One click each, or ⌘1 to ⌘4.

Skin & light

Smoothing that keeps texture, plus brightness and warmth.

Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac.

Glow does all of its work on your Mac. Your video and your face never leave it. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to share.

Read the privacy policy

  • No uploadsFrames are processed in memory and discarded. Nothing is sent to any server.
  • No face data keptFace points place the effects for one frame, then they are gone.
  • No account, no trackingNo sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code.
  • Camera off when idleYour camera runs only while Glow's window is open or a call uses Glow Camera.

Which apps can use Glow Camera?

Any app that lets you choose a camera: Zoom, Google Meet in your browser, Microsoft Teams, FaceTime, Slack, Webex, Discord, OBS and more.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No. You pay once in the Mac App Store. There is no account, no sign-in and no subscription.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Glow processes every frame on your Mac and keeps nothing. Your video app then sends the call as usual. See the privacy policy.

Do people on the call see it when I compare?

No. Compare changes only your preview. To turn all filters off for everyone, press ⌘E.

Glow Camera does not show up in my video app

Quit the video app and open it again. Apps that were open before Glow Camera was installed cannot see it yet. More help.
