Support

Answers to common questions about setting up and using Glow Camera.

Setup

Glow Camera does not appear in Zoom, Meet or Teams

A video app that was already open before Glow Camera was installed or updated cannot see it. Quit the app and open it again:

  1. Switch to the video app and press ⌘Q. Closing its window is not enough.
  2. Open the app again.
  3. In its camera or video settings, choose Glow Camera.

For Google Meet and other apps in Chrome, quit and reopen Chrome. To keep your tabs, type chrome://restart in the address bar and press Return.

Glow's window lists the apps that you need to restart.

If Glow Camera still does not appear, check that it is turned on (see the next question).

How do I turn on Glow Camera?

Glow Camera is a camera extension. macOS asks you to approve it the first time.

  • macOS 15 Sequoia and later: open System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions > Camera Extensions and turn on Glow.
  • macOS 14 Sonoma: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and allow the system extension from Glow.

Glow must be in your Applications folder. macOS installs camera extensions only from there.

Glow's setup checklist has a button that opens the right page in System Settings.

My video app shows "Open the Glow app to show your video"

Glow must be running for Glow Camera to show your video. Open Glow from your Applications folder.

To keep Glow ready, turn on Open Glow when you log in in Glow > Settings (⌘,). Glow then waits in the menu bar. You can close its window.

Glow Camera disappeared after an update

Sometimes macOS does not start the updated Glow Camera. Glow shows "Glow Camera did not start" in its window.

  1. Click Fix It in Glow's window.
  2. When macOS asks, turn on Glow again under System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions > Camera Extensions.
  3. Quit and reopen your video apps.

Restarting your Mac also fixes it.

Camera access is off

Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera and turn on Glow. If Glow is open, you may need to quit it and open it again.

Using Glow

Glow uses the wrong camera

Choose the camera you want from the camera menu in Glow's toolbar. Glow Camera then shows video from that camera.

My image looks blurred twice, or reactions appear

macOS has its own Video Effects, such as Portrait, Studio Light and Reactions. They can apply on top of Glow's effects.

To turn them off for Glow, open Control Center > Video Effects while Glow uses the camera, and turn off the effects you do not want.

Do other people see the original when I use Compare?

No. When you hold Space or the compare button, only your preview changes. People on the call keep seeing the filtered image. To turn all filters off for everyone, press ⌘E.

When is my camera on?

Glow turns your camera on only while the Glow window is open or a video app uses Glow Camera. The rest of the time the camera is off.

Removing Glow

How do I remove Glow Camera?
  1. Open Glow > Settings (⌘,) and click Remove Glow Camera.
  2. Quit Glow, then delete Glow from your Applications folder.

Contact support

If your question is not answered here, email us. A diagnostics report helps us find the problem faster:

  1. Open Glow > Settings (⌘,).
  2. Click Copy Diagnostics. This puts a short text report on your clipboard.
  3. Paste the report into an email and tell us what happened.

The report contains your Glow and macOS versions, Mac model, setup state, camera names, recent Glow log messages and crash report summaries. It never contains images. See the privacy policy for details.
