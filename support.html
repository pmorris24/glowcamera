Setup

Glow does not appear in Zoom, Meet or Teams

A video app that was already open before Glow was installed or updated cannot see it. Quit the app and open it again:

  1. Switch to the video app and press ⌘Q. Closing its window is not enough.
  2. Open the app again.
  3. In its camera or video settings, choose Glow.

For Google Meet and other apps in Chrome, quit and reopen Chrome. To keep your tabs, type chrome://restart in the address bar and press Return.

The Glow island at the top of your screen lists the apps that you need to restart.

If Glow still does not appear, check that it is turned on (see the next question).

How do I turn on the Glow camera?

The Glow camera is a camera extension. macOS asks you to approve it the first time.

  • macOS 15 Sequoia and later: open System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions > Camera Extensions and turn on Glow.
  • macOS 14 Sonoma: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and allow the system extension from Glow.

Glow must be in your Applications folder. macOS installs camera extensions only from there.

Glow's setup checklist has a button that opens the right page in System Settings.

My video app shows "Open the Glow app to show your video"

Glow must be running for Glow to show your video. Open Glow from your Applications folder.

To keep Glow ready, turn on Open Glow when you log in in Glow's Settings (click the gear in the island, or choose Settings… from the Glow menu bar icon). Glow then waits at the notch and in the menu bar.

The Glow camera disappeared after an update

Sometimes macOS does not start the updated Glow camera. The light on the Glow island turns orange, and the island shows "The Glow camera did not start".

  1. Move the pointer to the notch to open the island, then click Fix It.
  2. When macOS asks, turn on Glow again under System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions > Camera Extensions.
  3. Quit and reopen your video apps.

Restarting your Mac also fixes it.

Camera access is off

Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera and turn on Glow. If Glow is open, you may need to quit it and open it again.

Using Glow

Glow uses the wrong camera

Open the island at the notch and click the camera button at its top right. Choose the camera you want. Glow then shows video from that camera.

My image looks blurred twice, or reactions appear

macOS has its own Video Effects, such as Portrait, Studio Light and Reactions. They can apply on top of Glow's effects. Glow has its own version of each one, so you can turn the macOS ones off and use Glow's instead.

To turn them off for Glow, open Control Center > Video Effects while Glow uses the camera, and turn off the effects you do not want.

Where is Glow's window?

Glow has no window. It lives in an island around the notch at the top of your screen, next to the camera. On a Mac without a notch, the island is at the middle of the menu bar.

  • Move the pointer onto the notch to open the island. Click inside it, or click the pin, to keep it open.
  • Click outside the island or press Esc to close it.
  • The light on the closed island is gray when Glow is ready, green during a call, and orange when Glow needs you.
  • Click a round effect tile to turn that effect on and show its controls. Click the tile again to hide them.
My preview is mirrored, or my screen looks reversed

Click the mirror button on the island's preview to change it. Mirroring changes only your preview. People on the call always see you the right way round.

While Presenter Overlay is on, the preview is not mirrored, so the text on your screen reads the right way.

Presenter Overlay asks to record my screen

Presenter Overlay puts your screen in your video, so macOS asks once for permission to record it. Turn on Glow under System Settings > Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording, then click Try Again in the Presenter Overlay controls.

You can also click Choose… in the Presenter Overlay controls and pick what to share in the macOS sharing picker. Then macOS does not ask for this permission.

Glow records only what you choose, and only while Presenter Overlay is on and the camera is in use. Glow's own island is left out.

How do I share one window, one app or one browser tab?

In the Presenter Overlay controls, click Choose…. The macOS sharing picker opens. Choose an entire display, one window or one app. Entire Screen goes back to your main display.

macOS cannot share one browser tab by itself. Drag the tab out to its own window, then choose that window.

In the same controls, choose the corner for your bubble, its size, and Mirror me. Mirror me mirrors only you. Your screen always shows the right way round.

How do I start a reaction with my hands?

Turn on Reactions, then hold a gesture in front of the camera for about half a second: a heart with both hands for hearts, a thumbs up or down, a peace sign for balloons, two peace signs for confetti, two thumbs up for fireworks, two thumbs down for rain, or rock on with both hands for lasers. You can also click a reaction in the Reactions controls.

Edge Light is not on my external display

In the Edge Light controls, choose the display in the Display menu, or choose All Displays. Automatic uses the built-in display. Edge Light shows only while a camera is in use.

Do other people see the original when I use Compare?

No. When you hold the compare button on the island's preview, or hold Space while the island is open, only your preview changes. People on the call keep seeing the filtered image. To turn all filters off for everyone, press ⌘E.

When is my camera on?

Glow turns your camera on only while the Glow island is open (and for a few seconds after you close it) or a video app uses Glow. The rest of the time the camera is off.

Removing Glow

How do I remove the Glow camera?
  1. Open Glow's Settings (click the gear in the island, or choose Settings… from the Glow menu bar icon) and click Remove Camera Extension.
  2. Quit Glow, then delete Glow from your Applications folder.

Contact support

If your question is not answered here, email us. A diagnostics report helps us find the problem faster:

  1. Open Glow's Settings (click the gear in the island, or choose Settings… from the Glow menu bar icon).
  2. Click Copy Diagnostics. This puts a short text report on your clipboard.
  3. Paste the report into an email and tell us what happened.

The report contains your Glow and macOS versions, Mac model, setup state, camera names, recent Glow log messages and crash report summaries. It never contains images. See the privacy policy for details.
